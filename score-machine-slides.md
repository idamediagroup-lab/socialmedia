**Say:** Nobody's in the wrong room tonight. Underwriting doesn't read a file differently because of what you want at the end of it.

**SLIDE 10 — Let me be straight with you**

**SLIDE 11 — The chain**

## ACT 2 — WHY YOU GOT DENIED (12–21 · 9 min)

**SLIDE 12 — The pivot**
> # A score is not an approval.

**Say:** A score gets you *considered*. It does not get you approved. Those are two different events and almost nobody is taught the difference.

---

**SLIDE 13 — Three files**
> You don't have a credit score.
> You have three files.
>
> Experian · Equifax · TransUnion
>
> Different data. Different scores.

---

**SLIDE 14 — The question**
> # Which one did they pull?

**Say:** Think about your last denial. Right now. Which bureau did that lender pull? [Pause. Let it be uncomfortable.] Almost nobody in this room can answer that. And that is exactly the problem.

---

**SLIDE 15 — Why it matters**
> Your best file doesn't get you approved.
> The one they pulled does.

---

**SLIDE 16 — Credit decay**
> Every application leaves an inquiry.
> Every inquiry lowers what a bank will extend you.
>
> This is called **credit decay.**

**Say:** This is the part nobody tells you. It's not just that inquiries ding your score a few points. Inquiries lower the dollar amount a bank is willing to hand you — and that happens quietly, in the background, whether you got approved or not.

---

**SLIDE 17 — What decay looks like**
> Real file. 14 inquiries.
>
> # Approval odds: −$5,000

**Say:** Negative five thousand. This person isn't getting a smaller approval — they're not getting anything, from anyone, and their credit score never told them that. They'd have kept applying.

---

**SLIDE 18 — The line**
> # Forget about points.
> # It's about money.

---

**SLIDE 19 — So what actually happened**
> You applied.
> You didn't know if you qualified.
> You got denied.
> And the inquiry made the next one harder.

---

**SLIDE 20 — The spiral**
> Denied → inquiry → lower ceiling →
> denied → inquiry → lower ceiling →
> denied

**Say:** This is why it feels like it's getting worse even though you're trying harder. You are trying harder. Every attempt is costing you the next one.

---

**SLIDE 21 — The turn**
> # There is a way to know first.

# DEMO (30 min)

Live software. Slides off. Beat order:

1. **Upload a report** — no data entry, watch it populate
2. **Full AI Credit File Analysis** — the report, sorted and readable
3. **The Blueprint on a real file** — the ten lines, live
4. **Personals that don't match** — the cheapest fix, and nearly everyone has it
5. **Utilization** — a real account at 101%
6. **The paydown calculator** — set the target, get the number: $7–8 a week
7. **Negative items** — what's disputable and what isn't
8. **AI letters** — written per account, not templates
9. **Print + certified mail** — they don't touch it
10. **Progress Report + Score Timeline** — every removal tracked
11. **Bank + bureau matching** — which banks, which bureau they pull, what order
12. **A green file** — end on the fundable one

**Rules:** no live pulls — files pre-pulled hours ahead. Full-resolution screenshots of every beat as backup. Everything anonymized. Never demo a module you know is broken. If the platform stalls, move to screenshots without announcing it.
