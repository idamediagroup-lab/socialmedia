## 2. 🚨 The Headline Finding: Three Brands Are Running At Once

This is the single most important thing on the page, and it needs resolving before we design anything.

### Identity A — "Global Fusion" (older, lives in the Reels grid)
- Lockup: **HERE TODAY / RESTAURANT & BAR** with an **"HT" monogram**
- Descriptor: *"A Global Fusion Experience — LATIN • AFRICAN • CARIBBEAN • SOUTHERN"*
- Palette: **gold on black**, high-contrast, promo-flyer construction
- Voice: deal-driven — *"NATIONAL OYSTERS DAY — ONLY $12"*
- Reel captions: *"NEW GLOBAL FUSION RESTAURANT IN ATL"*, *"Jumbo Stuffed Lobster, Fried Cornish Hens & More!!"*

### Identity B — "Tapas & Cocktails" (newer, all four Story Highlights, dated June 12)
- Lockup: **HERE TODAY / — TAPAS & COCKTAILS —**
- Descriptor: *"A modern dining & nightlife experience"*
- Palette: **cream, terracotta/rust, antique gold, espresso brown**
- Motifs: orchid/magnolia line art, cartouche + arch frames, arrow-finial rules, filigree crests
- Voice: atmospheric, hospitality-led — *"stylish, social, and unforgettable"*

### Identity C — OpenTable
- *"Southern tapas"*, *"Southern and Southwestern"*

**Three different cuisine stories, two different logo lockups, two different palettes, one venue.** The bio itself splits the difference ("A modern dining & nightlife experience ✨ / Craft cocktails • Music • Late nights") and names no cuisine at all.

`[INFER]` This reads as a **rebrand in progress** — Identity B is newer (June), more considered, and now occupies the highlights, which are the profile's permanent furniture. The Reels grid is legacy inventory that hasn't been cleared. **First question for the client: is the Global Fusion identity retired, or still live?** Everything we design forks on that answer.

## 3. 🚨 The Location Story Is Wrong — And The Truth Is Better

**What they claim** `[IG]`: *"Located near Ponce City Market in the heart of Midtown Atlanta."*

**What's actually true** `[WEB]`:

- 736 Ponce De Leon Ave NE, **30306**, is the **Common Market** retail center (built 1986)
- That is **Poncey-Highland**, on the Virginia-Highland edge — **not Midtown**
- Ponce City Market is at 675 Ponce De Leon Ave NE, **30308** — close, but a different zip and a different neighborhood
- **The same 736 address houses MJQ Concourse, The Drunken Unicorn, and Friends on Ponce**

That last line is the finding. **Here Today shares a building with three established Atlanta nightlife institutions.** MJQ Concourse is a 20+ year basement dance-club fixture running deep into the night; The Drunken Unicorn is an indie music venue; Friends on Ponce is a long-running neighborhood gay bar.

### Why this matters to us
1. **It explains the 2:30 AM close.** They aren't a restaurant that stays open late — they're a restaurant **built into a late-night complex**. The hours are architecture, not ambition.
2. **There is a captive, pre-qualified crowd on-site every weekend night** already out, already spending, already within walking distance of the door.
3. **"Midtown" actively costs them search traffic.** Nobody searching *Midtown* converts here, and everyone searching *Poncey-Highland*, *Virginia-Highland*, *MJQ*, or *near Ponce City Market* fails to find them. It's a self-inflicted local-SEO wound in the bio and the Location highlight — the two places it's hardest to undo.
4. `[INFER]` **"Dinner that turns into a night out, without moving your car"** is a positioning nobody else on Ponce can claim. Their own copy already gestures at it — *"designed for dinners that turn into late nights"* — they just haven't connected it to the building.

⚠️ **NAP consistency risk:** listings at this complex split between "736 Ponce De Leon **Ave** NE" and "736 Ponce De Leon **Pl** NE." Whichever is correct needs to be identical across Google, OpenTable, Instagram, and anything we build.

### Typography `[INFER]` — identification by eye
- **Display:** high-contrast Didone-style serif — Playfair Display, Prata, or Cormorant family. Used for HOURS / LOCATION / OUR GUESTS / HERE TODAY.
- **Accent italic:** matching Didone italic — *"Open daily"*, *"Thank you"*
- **Body:** rounded geometric humanist sans — Poppins / Brandon Grotesque family
- **Lockup subtitle:** wide-letterspaced small caps — `TAPAS & COCKTAILS`

### Recurring motifs
- Orchid / magnolia botanical **line art** (not filled) — top and bottom anchors
- **Cartouche and arch frames** — scalloped, ogee-arched, rust-stroked
- **Arrow-finial horizontal rules** — a thin line terminating in small arrowheads
- **Plaque/badge containers** for hard facts (address, CTA, one-line payoffs)
- **Cream brick texture** as a light-mode ground; dark interior photography as a dark-mode ground
- Ornamental **filigree crests** as section headers

### Interior / photography direction `[IG]`
Shot from the Location highlight: red-shaded pendant lamps, bookshelves lined with red-spined books, **black-and-gold veined marble** tables, **rust velvet banquettes**, brass table edging, backlit slatted-wood walls. Food photography is dark, warm, and close — tacos on ceramic, red wine in stemless glass, a hanging empanada rack, oysters.

`[INFER]` The venue's own interior *is* the brand palette — rust, gold, black marble, warm low light. Any design we do should read as an extension of the room, not a layer on top of it.

### The gap
**~94K+ views across two reels against 1,724 followers — roughly 30:1.**

`[INFER]` **This is not a content problem. It's a conversion problem.** The content reaches; the profile doesn't close. Every one of those ~94K viewers who tapped through hit a profile with:
- **no link in bio** — no menu, no reservation, no map
- **no cuisine named** in the bio
- a **grid that contradicts the highlights** (gold-on-black flyers vs. cream-and-rust editorial)
- **no reason-to-follow** stated anywhere

Fixing the profile is higher-leverage than making more reels. The traffic already exists.

### What's working
- **Food-hero reels with a specific dish named in the caption** are the format that scales — the two 39K+ performers both do this. "Jumbo Stuffed Lobster" and "Fried Cornish Hens" are concrete, screenshot-able, and searchable.
- **Creator UGC is already in the mix** — the "Places to start your Night in Atlanta" reel is a third-party creator, and the follower list includes **@therealcocoabrown** (comedian/actress, significant Atlanta reach) and **@iamcorinthian**. There's an influencer channel here that's warm and underworked.
- **"Tag us to be featured"** — the Guests highlight already runs a UGC solicitation. Good instinct, no visible follow-through system.

## 6. Voice — How They Currently Sound `[IG]`

Verbatim from the highlights:

> "Here Today was created to bring together elevated dining and nightlife **in one experience.** A place where great food, craft cocktails, music, and energy come together from day to night."

> "Located near Ponce City Market, we're designed for dinners that turn into late nights: **stylish, social, and unforgettable**"

> "Welcome to your new Atlanta spot."

> "From elevated lunches and cocktails to late-night energy. We're here all day and into the night."

> "The energy, the moments, the people who make Here Today what it is. *Thank you* for being part of the experience."

**Characteristics:** warm, inclusive, hospitality-first. Leans on **"energy"** as its signature noun (appears in 4 of 5 highlight cards). Triadic adjective strings — *"stylish, social, and unforgettable."* Second person, welcoming. Zero hard-sell; even the CTA is soft ("Tag us to be featured").

**Missing from the voice:** any specific dish, price, chef, ingredient, or point of pride. It's all atmosphere and no substance — which is exactly inverse to what's actually driving their reach, where naming *"Jumbo Stuffed Lobster"* pulled 39K.

## 7. Competitive Context `[WEB]`

Ponce/PCM is one of Atlanta's densest dining corridors. Nearby and adjacent: 9 Mile Station, Madeira Park (Michelin-listed), the full PCM food hall, El Ponce, Bar.Bacoa, Buena Vida Tapas, Silla del Toro.

`[INFER]` **Here Today doesn't win on food credentials in that set** — Madeira Park has Michelin recognition; the PCM hall has volume and brand. **Here Today wins on the hours and the building.** At 1 PM–2:30 AM daily in a nightlife complex, the competitive set after ~11 PM shrinks to almost nothing. Most food-forward Ponce kitchens close by 11 PM or midnight. That late window is the defensible territory.

## 10. Where I'd Start `[INFER]`

Ranked by leverage, not effort:

1. **Pick one identity and kill the other.** Nothing else compounds until this is settled.
2. **Fix the bio.** Add a link (menu + OpenTable at minimum), name the cuisine, and drop "Midtown." This is a 20-minute change against ~94K of existing reach.
3. **Own Poncey-Highland and the late-night window.** Stop competing for "Midtown dinner" and start owning "the kitchen that's still open at 1 AM on Ponce."
4. **Build the reel format that already works** — one named dish, shot dark and close, dish name in the first caption line.
5. **Claim the rest of the footprint** — GBP, Yelp, TikTok, and a one-page site with a menu.
6. **Work the warm influencer list.** Cocoa Brown already follows them.
