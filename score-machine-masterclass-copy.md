## Section 10 — Proof

**Section headline**

> ## Real People. Real Approvals.

**Template — this is a shape, not copy. Every one of these must be a real
person who really said it.**

> "[SPECIFIC BEFORE STATE — score, denial, situation]. [WHAT CHANGED].
> [SPECIFIC RESULT WITH A NUMBER AND A TIMEFRAME]."
> **— [FULL NAME], [CITY] · [BUSINESS TYPE]**

**Target: 4–6 of these.** Ranked by what actually moves people on a funding page:

1. **Video** — 30 to 60 seconds, filmed on a phone, no editing. Highest trust by
   a wide margin in this niche, because the skepticism is highest.
2. **Screenshots** — approval letters and score dashboards with personal
   information blacked out. Beat any written quote you could write.
3. **Photo + full name + specific number.**
4. Text with a first name only ← weak. Use as filler, not as your lead.

**Rules:**
- Every one needs a number and a timeframe. "Nicholas changed my life" converts
  nobody. "Went from three denials to $[X] approved in 71 days" converts.
- Pick people whose *starting point* looks like your audience's, not your best
  outcome ever. The reader is looking for someone like them, not someone above
  them.
- Whatever results you show, "results not typical" goes in the footer.

## Voice notes for whoever builds this

**On-voice, and why:** short lines with breaks instead of paragraphs. Ellipsis
trails on two section headers (5 and 14). ALL CAPS on the three Secret names and
every button. Bold claims stated flat with no hedging. Second person throughout
— talking to one business owner at 11pm, not presenting to a room.

**Deliberately different from the social voice:** the CTA here is the button,
not `Comment "AI"`. That trigger belongs on Instagram, Facebook, and Threads
posts. Putting it on a landing page competes with the only action this page
exists to produce. Same voice, different ask.

**One thing to watch:** `brand-voice.md` is built around the AI-and-automation
offer, and this is a capital offer. The voice transfers cleanly. The *proof*
does not — the $1M credit-repair-and-funding-coach story in Section 7 of
`brand-voice.md` is proof that Nicholas builds systems that print money, not
proof that he gets people funded. Don't repurpose it as a funding testimonial on
this page. It's a great story for the class itself, told as what it is.

## Before this goes live

**Fill in every bracket.** In priority order:

1. **The host story (Section 9).** The page cannot launch without it. Four
   beats, real numbers.
2. **Testimonials (Section 10).** Four to six real ones. Screenshots beat text.
3. **Date, day, time, timezone.** Appears in five places — bar, hero, urgency,
   final CTA, and the class itself.
4. **Credibility strip numbers (Section 4)** — or delete the section.
5. **The four deliverables (Section 11)** — build them or cut them.
6. **Legal entity name and the three legal pages.**

**Two compliance realities worth knowing before you spend a dollar** — flagging
these because they're cheap to handle now and expensive to handle after a ban:

- **Meta treats credit as a Special Ad Category.** Ads about credit
  opportunities — including business funding, loans, and credit-building offers
  — have to be declared as such, and declaring it strips most of your detailed
  targeting: no age or gender targeting, restricted location radius, limited
  interest options. This directly changes the five-audience structure in the
  `paidads` skill. Plan the audience mix around lookalikes and broad, because
  interest stacking mostly won't be available to you here.
- **Keep guarantees off the page.** No promised approval amounts, no "guaranteed
  funding," no promise to remove accurate negative items. Section 8 already says
  this out loud, which is both honest and good copy. If the backend program
  includes credit repair services, the Credit Repair Organizations Act adds real
  requirements — written contract, three-day cancellation, no fees collected
  before services are performed. Worth ten minutes with a lawyer before launch.

Neither of these blocks the page. Both are much easier to build in now than to
retrofit after an ad account gets restricted.
