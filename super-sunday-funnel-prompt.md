**Everything below the line is the prompt. Copy from `═══ SUPER SUNDAY ═══` all
the way to the bottom and paste it anywhere.** It carries the full page copy
inside it, so whoever gets it — an AI, a designer, a VA — has everything and
doesn't have to invent a word.

Replace every `[BRACKET]` with a real fact before this goes live. The list is at

═══ SUPER SUNDAY — FUNNEL BUILD PROMPT ═══

You are building the home page for a free live masterclass registration funnel.
All of the copy is written for you below. Use it exactly as written. Do not
rewrite it, soften it, shorten it, or "improve" it.

───────────────────────────────────
THE POSITIONING (locked)
───────────────────────────────────
The page leads with FUNDING, not credit scores.

Nobody wakes up wanting a 740. They want the money a 740 unlocks. Funding is
the promise. The credit score is the mechanism. That is exactly why the brand
is called The Score Machine — it's the machine that turns a number into money.

The class itself covers personal credit, business credit, and how to run a
credit repair business. The PAGE only sells one idea:

    You were denied for a reason nobody told you. That reason is knowable,
    and it's fixable in 90 days.

Every section supports that. Nothing contradicts it.

───────────────────────────────────
WHO IT'S FOR
───────────────────────────────────
Business owners and solopreneurs under $1M a year who:
  · Got denied and were never told the real reason
  · Have a "fine" score — 680, 700 — and still got capped at $2,000
  · Have paid a credit repair company monthly for the better part of a year
    and barely moved
  · Are running the business on a personal card at 29% and calling it capital
  · Have the customers and the contracts, not the cash for the next one

Write to ONE of them. At 11pm. After a denial. Not to an audience.

───────────────────────────────────
THE VOICE (Nicholas Minter)
───────────────────────────────────
· First person. Talking TO one business owner, never presenting AT a room.
· Short lines with breaks between thoughts. Never paragraphs. Reads like a
  text message, not an article. Fragments are fine.
· Maximum bold. Claims stated flat. Never hedge — no "maybe," no "this might
  help," no "consider."
· Trailing multi-dot ellipses to open a thought: "Let's see......."
· ALL CAPS for signature lines and every button.
· Stakes-forward. Naming what doing nothing costs is on-brand.
· Direct, a little confrontational. Tell them what's wrong, then tell them
  exactly what to do next.
· Never: religion, crypto, feuds, or naming a competitor. Calling out bad
  PRACTICES is fine. Naming the company doing it is not.

BUILD RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
· Four buttons minimum down the page (hero, section 7, section 10, section 14).
  EVERY button says the same words and does the same thing. No secondary CTA,
  no "learn more," no "book a call."
· Sticky mobile CTA bar that appears once the hero scrolls out of view.
· Mobile first. Headline, subhead and button must fit on a phone screen with no
  scrolling. Tap targets at least 44px. Full-width buttons on mobile.
· One accent color, used ONLY on buttons. The moment that color shows up in a
  heading, the button stops reading as clickable.
· Alternating section backgrounds — white / off-white — to separate sections
  without drawing lines. One dark section for emphasis, use it on section 7.
· Section spacing 64–96px desktop, 40–56px mobile. Content max width ~1150px,
  text columns 600–700px.
· Mobile type: headline 28–34px, section headings 22–26px, body 16–18px.
· NO navigation menu. NO outbound links. NO social icons. NO price anywhere on
  this page — the page sells the free class, the class sells the program.
· Page target: under 2.5 seconds to interactive on 4G.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HARD RULE ON FACTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
DO NOT INVENT ANY FACT. Not a testimonial, not a client, not a number, not a
credential, not a date, not a student count, not a dollar amount.

Every bracket above is a real fact that has to be supplied by the business.
Leave the brackets in place rather than filling them with something plausible.
Fabricated proof on a money page is worse than no proof.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
COMPLIANCE — NON-NEGOTIABLE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
· No guaranteed approvals. No promised funding amounts. No "guaranteed
  results." No promise to remove accurate negative items from a credit report.
· "Up to" and "results vary" framing anywhere an outcome is implied.
· Results disclaimer and Meta non-affiliation line stay in the footer exactly
  as written.
· Note for whoever runs the ads: Meta treats credit — including business
  funding and credit-building offers — as a Special Ad Category. It has to be
  declared, and declaring it strips most detailed targeting: no age, no gender,
  restricted location radius, limited interests. Plan the audience mix around
  broad and lookalike, not interest stacking.
