# The Art of Viral Carousels

A design and structure playbook for Instagram carousels. Follow these rules for every carousel posted as Nicholas Minter (pairs with brand-voice.md — voice always wins on copy; this file governs structure and design).

---

## 1. Carousel Size

| Ratio | Dimensions | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1:1 | 1080 × 1080 | ❌ Don't post this — too much wasted space |
| 4:5 | 1080 × 1350 | ⚠️ You *could* use this — best if you plan to boost the post |
| 3:4 | 1080 × 1440 | ✅ This is the best — maximum feed real estate |

---

## 2. Carousel Design (Typography)

- **Headline:** 50pt or more (55pt is a good default)
- **Body copy:** 14pt or more
- Anything smaller causes people to stop reading. Zoom in and sanity-check your smallest text before publishing.

---

## 3. Cover Safe Zone

Keep all critical text and focal elements inside these margins on the cover slide:

- **180px** top
- **180px** bottom
- **50px** left
- **120px** right

> Note: Carousels with music attached also appear in the Reels tab — so the safe zone matters even more.

---

## 4. Carousel Anatomy — the A.I.D.A. Structure

A 10-slide carousel mapped to **A**ttention → **I**nterest → **D**etail → **A**ction.

### Slide 1 — THE HOOK *(Attention)*
**Where you stop the scroll.**

Pattern: bold contrarian claim + one striking image.

Formats that work:
- "[Thing everyone relies on] IS DEAD" paired with a stark visual (skull, wreckage, decay)
- "STOP DOING [common practice] WRONG" paired with a literal symbol (stop sign, warning light)
- A single provocative statement in oversized condensed type over a high-contrast photo

---

### Slide 2 — THE TRANSITION *(Interest)*
Answer one of three questions:
1. What will the user **gain**?
2. What will the user **avoid**?
3. What makes **you qualified**?

Formats that work:
- Lead with your own result, then promise the reason behind it: "I did X and got Y — here's why most people fail 👉" *(qualification)*
- Open a curiosity gap and cut off mid-sentence: "It's a well-known fact that people don't read long text. Instead, they 👉"
- State the payoff bluntly: "THIS WILL KEEP YOU SCROLLING" *(promise)*

---

### Slides 3–7 — THE TEASE *(Interest / build-up)*
**Keep people swiping by revealing info bit by bit.** Use examples, statistics, and captivating visuals.

Execution ideas:
- Numbered format cards with rating bars (viral potential, posting frequency, difficulty score, goal of format, repurpose score)
- Simple comparison slides — e.g. test new topics in Stories first, use proven concepts in the Feed
- "#4 [TACTIC NAME] — What it is / How to use it" with a short numbered breakdown and a supporting screenshot

---

### Slides 8–9 — THE CLIMAX *(Detail)*
**The "Aha moment."** The big reveal of the story, lesson, quotable moment, and practical insight.

Execution ideas:
- A dense reference chart people will save — e.g. a color-psychology grid mapping palettes (earth, jewel, monochromatic, gradient, desaturated) to feelings (calm, luxury, cohesion, modern, maturity)
- A quotable line placed in an unexpected object — text typed onto an old phone screen, a sign, a receipt
- A side-by-side teardown: "UGLY" vs "VIRAL" version of the same asset with annotated callouts on both

---

### Slide 10 — THE ACTION *(Action)*
**Make the next step obvious.** One clear ask, one keyword, no ambiguity.

Formats that work:
- Personal confession → result → ask: "I've always hated [task]… yet I grew by doubling down on what works. COMMENT '[KEYWORD]' to learn how."
- Interrupt mid-carousel: "Before we continue… want to learn [outcome]? COMMENT '[KEYWORD]'"
- Outcome question → waitlist: "Want [specific result]? COMMENT '[KEYWORD]' & sign up for the waitlist"

---

## 5. The Offer Slide (final CTA anatomy)

A closing slide that converts usually stacks these five elements:

- **Credibility line:** how much time/work went into the thing being offered
- **The ask (huge):** COMMENT "[KEYWORD]" TO LEARN MORE
- **Product mockup** on a laptop or phone, with four benefit callouts on lines radiating out, e.g.:
  - Number of templates included
  - Page count of the written material (and which tools it's built for)
  - Length of video training
  - Size of the community
- **Urgency badge** inside the mockup: "PRICE DOUBLES IN 5 DAYS" or similar
- **Handle/branding** in a consistent header bar

---

## 6. Visual Style Reference (Nicholas's preferred look)

Based on approved reference designs:

- Surreal, editorial photo-composite covers: deep blue sky with white clouds, sculptural/classical figure with a modern tech twist (e.g., device worn as a visor)
- Oversized ultra-bold condensed sans-serif headlines in white (covers) or black (inner slides)
- One accent word per headline in a pink→orange gradient or brush-stroke style
- Inner slides: clean white background, rounded-corner outline cards, generous whitespace
- Consistent header bar on every slide: niche label + @handle
- Progress dots at the bottom; arrow affordance on the right edge

> For Nicholas: header bar = "AI Systems & Automation | @nicholasminter" (adjust to actual handle), accent gradient on the single most loaded word of each headline.

## Quick Checklist

- [ ] 1080 × 1440 (3:4)
- [ ] Headline ≥ 50pt, body ≥ 14pt
- [ ] Cover text inside the safe zone (180/180/50/120)
- [ ] Slide 1 stops the scroll with a bold claim
- [ ] Slide 2 states the gain, the avoid, or your credentials
- [ ] Slides 3–7 drip the value out, never dump it
- [ ] Slides 8–9 deliver the quotable payoff
- [ ] Slide 10 gives one unmistakable next step
