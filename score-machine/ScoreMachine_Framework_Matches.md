## Concept 1: The Bottleneck Is the Read-Through

### Frameworks considered (8)

| # | Framework | Library | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Problem Diagnosis** | post-structures | ✅ STRONG | "The reason you can't take more clients is because of: [components]" — the concept literally decomposes into named causes. |
| 2 | **The Hard Truth Post** | post-structures | ✅ STRONG | "Hard pill: your skill isn't the constraint, your clerical work is" lands as a callout without insulting the reader's expertise. |
| 3 | **State your BEFORE and AFTER Transformation** (#85) | linkedin | ✅ STRONG | Workflow before/after is visual and concrete — no client data needed to make it real. |
| 4 | **Time Management Truth** | post-structures | ⚠️ MODERATE | On-topic but generic; risks sounding like productivity filler rather than credit-specific. |
| 5 | **The "How to Save Time" Post** (#51) | linkedin | ⚠️ MODERATE | Fits, but the obvious pick — exactly the first-match trap. Skipped deliberately. |
| 6 | **Contrast and Solution Hook** | post-structures | ⚠️ MODERATE | Works, but overlaps Problem Diagnosis and is better spent on Concept 5. |
| 7 | **The Formula For X Benefit** | post-structures | ❌ WEAK | Additive-habits format doesn't match a subtractive "remove the bottleneck" idea. |
| 8 | **Fun Game/Trivia Posts** (#28) | linkedin | ❌ WEAK | Wrong register for a workflow-pain concept. |

### SELECTED
1. **State your BEFORE and AFTER Transformation** → LinkedIn (POST 3) — concrete, needs no invented metrics, screenshot-friendly.
2. **The Hard Truth Post** → held for round 2. Problem Diagnosis is the stronger structure for this concept, but Concept 2 needs it more and a batch shouldn't run the same template twice.

## Concept 2: Invisible Results Cause Churn

### Frameworks considered (7)

| # | Framework | Library | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cause and Effect** | post-structures | ✅ STRONG | Churn ← invisibility is a clean causal chain. |
| 2 | **Problem Diagnosis** | post-structures | ✅ STRONG | Splits cleanly into visibility / proof / language, and the "the reason X is because Y" opening puts the churn cause in line one. Strongest fit in the batch — claimed here over Concept 1. |
| 3 | **The "This vs That" Post** (#55) | linkedin | ⚠️ MODERATE | Binary works but flattens a three-part cause. |
| 4 | **Counterintuitive Statement Hook** | post-structures | ⚠️ MODERATE | "Your clients aren't leaving over results" is genuinely counterintuitive — good hook, thin body. |
| 5 | **The "Screenshot" Post** (#11) | linkedin | ⚠️ MODERATE | Strong *if* you have a real timeline screenshot to show. Held for when you do. |
| 6 | **Heavy Hitting One Liner** | post-structures | ✅ STRONG | Compresses to an X-length line cleanly. |
| 7 | **The "Comedy" Post** (#26) | linkedin | ❌ WEAK | Churn is a money wound; jokes cheapen it. |

### SELECTED
1. **Problem Diagnosis** → LinkedIn (POST 1).
2. **Heavy Hitting One Liner** → X (POST 4).
3. **Screenshot Post** → parked until a real progress-report screenshot is available.
